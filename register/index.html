Complete Your Child's Enrollment

We're so glad you toured with us. Here's everything you need to secure your child's spot and get ready for their first day. To secure your child's spot, a $300 cash payment is required.

Your Enrollment Checklist

1

Complete the online registration & the paper registration packet

Both are required. First, fill out the online registration below. It takes about 10 minutes and goes straight to our office. Then complete the registration packet you received after your tour and bring it with you when you drop off your documents. Lost it? No problem. Download and reprint it below, or fill it out and email it to us at info@creativaacademy.com. The supply list tells you what your child will need for their first day.

Start online registration ↓ Registration Packet 2026–27 (required) Supply List 2026–27
2

Gather your documents

Bring these with your completed registration packet. Copies are fine — you do not need to leave originals.

What to bring

  • Child's original birth certificate (copy is fine)
  • Florida Certificate of Immunization (Florida Form 680)
  • Child's physical / health exam (completed within the last year)
  • VPK certificate of eligibility — if applying for VPK (see Step 3)
  • Step Up or School Readiness award letter — if applicable

Registration fee: $300 cash

To secure your child's spot, a $300 cash payment is required. It is paid when you drop off your documents. Your child's place is held once we receive it.

3

Apply for financial aid & scholarships (if applicable)

Florida offers tuition assistance that many families qualify for. If you haven't applied yet, now is the time — spots fill up once families have their certificates.

Programs available

VPK (Voluntary Prekindergarten) — Available to every Florida 4-year-old. Apply at elcmdm.org. VPK covers a large portion of tuition; a remaining balance applies. Creativa is an approved VPK provider.

Step Up for Students — Income-based scholarship for students with disabilities and other qualifying criteria. Apply at stepupforstudents.org.

School Readiness (SR) — Income-based childcare subsidy also managed by the Early Learning Coalition. Apply at elcmdm.org. Some families use both VPK and SR together.

Creativa Academy does not guarantee eligibility, approval, or funding amounts. Contact the relevant program directly to confirm your status.

4

Send your registration & drop off your documents

Once you've sent the online registration below, stop by the school any time we're open and bring your completed packet, your documents and the $300 cash registration fee. No appointment needed, and there's no need to wait for our reply. Just text or call Emily at (786) 343-1795 to let us know you're on your way. Your child's spot is held as soon as we receive the fee.
