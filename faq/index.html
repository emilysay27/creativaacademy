Enrollment & Getting Started

How do I enroll my child at Creativa Academy?
The first step is to schedule a tour. During the tour you'll meet the director, see the classrooms, and ask all your questions. After the tour, if you'd like to move forward, we'll walk you through the enrollment packet and registration process step by step.
What ages do you serve?
We welcome children from 15 months through Kindergarten. Our programs include 15–17 months, 18–23 months, Early & Late Twos, Early & Late Threes, VPK (Pre-K), and Kindergarten.
When is enrollment open?
We are currently enrolling for the 2027–2028 school year. Enrollment is rolling and spaces fill throughout the year. We recommend touring and registering early — especially for infant and toddler rooms, which fill quickly. A registration fee of $300 (cash only) holds your child's spot.
Do you have a waitlist?
Yes. If the program your child needs is currently full, you can join our waitlist. We will contact you as soon as a space becomes available. There is no fee to join the waitlist.
Can my child visit before their first day?
Absolutely. We encourage a visit or short "trial morning" before the first official day — it helps children feel familiar with the environment and eases the transition. Talk to us during your tour or call us at (786) 900-0099 to arrange one.

Programs & Daily Schedule

What is the Reggio Emilia approach?
Reggio Emilia is a child-centered educational philosophy that treats children as capable, curious, and full of ideas. At Creativa, children lead their own learning through projects, exploration, and open-ended play while teachers act as collaborators and observers. The environment itself is considered a "third teacher" — carefully designed to invite discovery and spark wonder.
What are your hours of operation?
Our full-day program runs Monday–Friday. Please call us at (786) 900-0099 or contact us for specific hours by program, as times may vary by age group and enrollment type.
What enrichment activities are included?
All programs include monthly music class, monthly yoga, and a monthly librarian-led story time tied to the classroom theme — all built into the school day. Children also have outdoor time every day (weather permitting). Throughout the day, learning is hands-on and project-based, weaving art, curiosity, and play together. We hold English circle time at 9:00 AM and Spanish circle time at 3:00 PM (after nap time), giving children a natural bilingual rhythm each day.
Do you offer VPK? Is it really free?
Yes! Creativa is an approved VPK provider. Florida's VPK program covers a significant portion of tuition — there is a remaining balance that families pay, but it is typically much less than the full tuition rate. VPK is available to every Florida child who turns 4 by September 1st, with no income requirement. To use it, apply through the Early Learning Coalition of Miami-Dade & Monroe and bring your certificate of eligibility to us. Learn more about VPK and other scholarships.
What is your student-to-teacher ratio?
We maintain small class sizes to ensure every child receives personalized attention. Ratios vary by age group in accordance with Florida state licensing requirements, and we often exceed those minimums. Ask during your tour for specific numbers by classroom.

Meals & Nutrition

Are meals included?
Yes. We provide breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack every school day. Creativa has its own in-house chef — we do not use catering. Every meal is cooked fresh at school daily, so your child is eating a home-cooked meal, not packaged or pre-made food. Meals are balanced, wholesome, and kid-friendly. You do not need to pack a lunch.
Can you accommodate food allergies or dietary restrictions?
Yes. Please let us know about any allergies or dietary needs during enrollment and we will work with you and our kitchen team to make accommodations. The safety of our children is always the priority.
Where can I see the weekly menu?
The monthly menu is available on our Menu page. You can also see it posted in your child's classroom each week.

Financial Aid & Scholarships

What scholarships does Creativa accept?
We proudly accept Florida's major early learning scholarships: VPK (covers a large portion of tuition for Florida 4-year-olds), FES (Family Empowerment Scholarship), Step Up For Students, and School Readiness. Many families attend Creativa at little or no cost through these programs. See all options and check eligibility.
How do I apply for a scholarship?
Each scholarship program has its own application process. We guide every family through the steps — from gathering documents to submitting your application. Start by scheduling a tour, and we will explain which programs your family may qualify for.
Does Creativa help with the scholarship application?
Yes. We will walk you through every step and answer your questions along the way. We also have a Family Support resource for families who need additional guidance navigating state programs.
What is the private tuition if we don't qualify for a scholarship?
Our tuition rates vary by program and schedule. Please visit our Tuition & Financial Aid page for the full rate table, or call us at (786) 900-0099 and we will be happy to walk you through the options.

Safety & Communication

How do you keep children safe?
Safety is woven into everything at Creativa. All staff are background-checked and first-aid certified. Our facility is secured with controlled access, and we follow strict sign-in/sign-out protocols. We conduct regular safety drills and maintain current state licensing at all times.
How will I know what my child did each day?
Teachers communicate daily through notes, verbal updates at pickup, and our parent communication app. You'll know what your child ate, what they worked on, and any highlights from the day. We also post photos and updates regularly so you can follow along even while you're at work.
What happens if my child is sick?
Children who are ill should stay home to protect the health of the whole community. If a child becomes sick during the day, we will contact you immediately to arrange pickup. We follow Florida's childcare licensing guidelines on illness and exclusion policies.
What is your policy on school closures?
We notify families of any closures or early dismissals via our parent communication app, text, and by posting a banner at the top of this website. We also follow Miami-Dade County emergency closure procedures for severe weather events.

Parents Night Out

What is Parents Night Out?
Parents Night Out (PNO) is an exclusive Saturday evening program where your child enjoys dinner, creative activities, games, music, and supervised fun with Creativa's own teachers — while you get a well-deserved night off. Sessions run Saturdays from 5:00–10:00 PM. Learn more and book a spot.
Is Parents Night Out only for enrolled families?
PNO is open to all families — you do not need to be an enrolled Creativa family to attend. It's a wonderful way to introduce your child to our school and teachers before making an enrollment decision.
How much does Parents Night Out cost?
The first child is $100 per session, and each additional sibling is $30. This all-inclusive rate covers dinner, dessert, activities, and full supervision. Payment is collected at check-in on the evening of your reservation.
How do I book a spot for Parents Night Out?
Visit our Parents Night Out page to see available dates and complete the reservation form. Spots are limited, so we recommend booking early.
