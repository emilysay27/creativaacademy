VPK in Florida: Everything Miami Parents Need to Know

By the Creativa Academy Team · November 15, 2026 · 6 min read

Florida is one of the few states in the country that funds Pre-K for every 4-year-old — regardless of income. The program is called VPK (Voluntary Prekindergarten), and it can save families a significant amount on tuition each year.

Yet many families in Miami don't realize they qualify, don't know how to apply, or assume it's complicated. It isn't. Here's everything you need to know.

What Is VPK?

VPK stands for Voluntary Prekindergarten Education Program. It's a Florida state program that provides a tuition certificate for one school year of Pre-K for every child who turns 4 by September 1st of that school year. It works at private schools like Creativa Academy — not just public schools.

The word "voluntary" means parents are not required to enroll their child. At Creativa, VPK covers a large portion of tuition — there is a remaining balance families pay, but it is considerably less than the full tuition rate.

Who Qualifies?

Eligibility is simple:

  • Your child must be 4 years old on or before September 1st of the school year
  • Your child must be a Florida resident
  • That's it — there is no income requirement

VPK is universal. A family earning $30,000 a year qualifies the same as a family earning $300,000. It is simply a benefit available to all Florida 4-year-olds.

What Does VPK Cover?

VPK provides a state-funded certificate that covers a significant portion of tuition at an approved Pre-K provider. At Creativa Academy, families who use VPK pay a remaining balance — it is not fully covered, but VPK brings the cost down considerably compared to full tuition.

What VPK does not cover: extended care hours before or after the VPK instructional day, meals, or enrichment extras like music or Spanish. Those are available as add-ons. Many families find that even with add-ons, VPK makes Creativa significantly more affordable than they expected.

How to Apply: Step by Step

The process takes about 15 minutes online.

Step 1: Get your child's certificate of eligibility. Go to the Early Learning Coalition of Miami-Dade & Monroe website (elcmdm.org) and complete the online eligibility application. You'll need your child's birth certificate and proof of Florida residency (a utility bill or lease works).

Step 2: Receive your certificate. Once approved — usually within a few days — you'll receive a VPK certificate of eligibility.

Step 3: Choose your provider. You can use your VPK certificate at any state-approved VPK provider. Creativa Academy is a licensed and approved VPK provider in Miami-Dade County.

Step 4: Enroll. Bring your certificate to your chosen school and complete their enrollment paperwork. The school handles the rest with the state directly.

When Should You Apply?

VPK certificate applications open in the spring for the following school year (typically March or April). Spots at popular private providers like Creativa fill up quickly once families have their certificates in hand.

Our recommendation: get your certificate as early as possible in the spring, then tour schools while spots are still open. Families who wait until August often find their preferred providers are full.

What About School Readiness (Childcare Assistance)?

VPK is different from School Readiness, which is an income-based childcare subsidy program also administered by the Early Learning Coalition. Some families qualify for both. If you think you might qualify for income-based assistance, it's worth asking the Early Learning Coalition about School Readiness at the same time you apply for VPK.

At Creativa, we work with families who have both VPK certificates and School Readiness vouchers. Our administrative team can help you understand how both programs work together — just call us or ask during your tour.

VPK at Creativa Academy

Creativa's VPK program runs within our standard school day alongside our Reggio Emilia curriculum. Children in VPK receive the same rich learning environment as all Creativa students — the weekly themes, the art studio, the bilingual classroom experience, and the small group ratios our families value.

Using your VPK certificate at Creativa is one of the best ways to access a high-quality early education at significantly reduced cost. We're here to help you navigate the process.
